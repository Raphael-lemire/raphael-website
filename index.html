Most frequent questions

Questions people usually ask before they make a move.

How does the Greater Moncton real estate market look right now?

It depends on the area, price range, property type, and current inventory. For regular local updates, subscribe to the market update. For advice on your specific situation, let's get in touch.

What should I expect when we first talk?

A simple conversation about your goals, timeline, budget or property, and the next practical step. There is no commitment.

Does it cost anything to talk?

No. It is free to talk through your options, whether it is the first conversation or a follow-up. If you decide to move forward, I will explain the process before anything changes.

What does BGRS approved mean?

BGRS handles many relocation moves, including military, RCMP, and federal employee relocations. Being approved means I can help clients who need extra care with timelines, paperwork, and process.
